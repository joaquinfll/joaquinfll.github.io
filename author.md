# Author
Hi, I'm Joaquin.

For most of my career, I have worked as a system administrator in environments where there was a clear segregation between developers and sysops.
In 2017 I had the chance to join an organization starting a cloud-native transformation journey. There, I started with agile practices and worked more closely with
developers and platform users, applying DevSecOps and Platform Engineering principles helping shape their OpenShift platform through the use of best practices for automation, cluster deployment, application and user onboarding, security, and resiliency.
